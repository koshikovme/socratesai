Dear Editor,

This submission is a substantially extended journal version of the earlier IEEE SIST conference paper titled "Real-Time Virtual Mentor for Introductory Programming Feedback." The conference version was a short systems paper focused mainly on the initial architecture and a smaller pilot trace.

The IJEP manuscript extends the conference version in several ways:

- It reframes the work for engineering pedagogy rather than only for a software-systems conference.
- It expands the literature review around automated feedback, intelligent tutoring, and LLM-based programming assistants.
- It adds a fuller explanation of the pedagogical policy layer, learner-state representation, and feedback-action design.
- It includes the architecture and interface diagrams in the manuscript.
- It updates the pilot evaluation from the earlier 78-event trace to 517 event-level records.
- It adds a clearer discussion of limitations, classroom implications, and future validation requirements.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. The prior conference paper is disclosed here so that the relationship between the two versions is transparent.
